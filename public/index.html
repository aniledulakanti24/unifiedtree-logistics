Moving goods across the city shouldn't mean juggling 4 different apps, dealing with unexpected surge pricing, or guessing vehicle payload limits.

The Fragmented Approach

Juggling Multiple Delivery Apps

  • ✕ App jumping: Constantly checking Porter, Rapido, couriers, and local contacts across separate browser tabs and mobile apps.
  • ✕ Weight rejections: Booking a 2-wheeler only for the rider to cancel on arrival because the parcel exceeds 5 kg.
  • ✕ Surge pricing surprises: Paying 1.8x to 2.5x surge during peak hours without knowing if a cheaper courier was nearby.
  • ✕ Cash bargaining: Local tempo drivers calling to demand arbitrary return charges or cash fuel surcharges.
Outcome: ~45 minutes wasted, uncertain arrival, and higher delivery costs.
The UT Logistics Approach

Single Search. Instant Vehicle Match.

  • ✓ Unified network: Enter pickup and drop once to query independent tempos, bikes, and courier networks simultaneously.
  • ✓ Smart payload matching: Automatic matching maps your exact package weight to the smallest economical vehicle.
  • ✓ Guaranteed transparent fares: Locked upfront rates with direct fleet pricing and zero hidden surge penalties.
  • ✓ Secure dispatch: Assigned verified driver within 90 seconds, live GPS tracking, and two-party OTP verification.
Outcome: Booking confirmed in under 2 minutes with up to 35% cost savings.
