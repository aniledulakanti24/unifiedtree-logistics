# UT Log compared with competitors

Reviewed November 2026 from public product pages, app listings and press. No competitor has one product covering all of these areas, so each is compared where it is strongest. "Demo" means the feature works end to end in UT Log, but on simulated data, because no provider is connected yet.

## Added in this round

- **Transport:** extra stops, scheduled pickup, deliver-by deadline, loading helpers, opt-in insurance, on-time record, side-by-side compare, book again
- **After booking:** in-app live tracking with OTP, problem reports and insurance claims, ratings, printable receipts
- **Travel:** trains, 14-day fare calendar, price alerts
- **Money:** coupons, reward points and tiers, budgets, spending insights, upcoming payments
- **Search:** plain-language search on the dashboard and in Ctrl K

## Still behind, in priority order

1. **Real provider connections.** Everything else depends on this. Competitors have live rates and real couriers.
2. **Native mobile apps.** Porter, Rapido, PhonePe and ixigo are mobile-first. UT Log is a responsive website.
3. **Real courier performance data.** Shiprocket's advantage comes from its shipment volume. UT Log can only collect this once real bookings flow.
4. **Price lock and flexible rebooking** for travel, which need airline or aggregator agreements.
5. **Per-employee spend rules and approvals** for business accounts.
6. **Packers and movers**, and voice search.
7. **Ride safety** such as an SOS button. It was left out on purpose: a safety feature must connect to a real emergency response, and a simulated one could put someone at risk.
